Motivation tips

Rule of 5 Minutes

Just start for five minutes. Most days you will keep going; if not, you still showed up.

Find Your Tribe

Train with a friend, family or a class. Shared plans are easier to keep.

Consistency over Intensity

Regular, comfortable sessions beat rare all-out efforts.

FAQ

I have no time

Split your session into three 10-minute "movement snacks": morning, midday and evening.

My muscles are sore

Mild soreness is normal. Keep moving gently, stretch, drink water and sleep well. Pain that is sharp or lasts is a reason to rest and see a professional.

I'm bored

Swap activities, add music, invite a friend or try a new outdoor route.
